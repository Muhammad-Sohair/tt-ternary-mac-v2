**What the violators are.** Small cells (nor2_2, xor2_2, a221oi_2, nand4_1) driving 0.03–0.06 pF, i.e.
100–200 µm wires: the `u_core.dot[r][c]` bits of the edge rows (0, 1, 5, 7) to their accumulators, and a few
decoder nets. Twice the rows on 1.5× the area spreads each row's logic further from its flops.

**Why the flow misses them.** Post-GRT `repair_design` (slew margin 55 %, i.e. it repairs to 0.34 ns) ends
with 0 remaining, yet the same nets measure up to 1.2 ns after detailed routing and RCX. Worst net in
wire200: `_03411_`, driver `_08422_` nand4_1 at x = 457 µm, load `fanout78` (a buffer repair inserted) placed
at x = 318 µm: ~150 µm of met3, under the 200 µm wire limit, and the nand4_1 was never upsized. So the
repair's view of these nets is wrong by > 3×, not slightly short of margin: more margin does not reach them.
The cause inside OpenROAD is [UNKNOWN] without re-running the step locally.

**Wire 100 µm** is not routable in the time the runner allows, so the wire-limit knob tops out near 200 µm.

**Density 72 %** made it worse (placement cannot reach a target above utilisation, and the extra squeeze
only lengthens other routes). Dropped.
